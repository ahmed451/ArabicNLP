Mona T. Diab is Director of the Language Technologies Institute and a Full Professor in the School of Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University. An ACL Fellow, she has devoted more than 13 years of her career to Arabic NLP and received the 2023 King Salman Global Academy for Arabic Language prize for advancing Arabic language technologies. She is a founding member of SIGARAB, a former President of SIGDAT and SIGLEX, and a cofounder of the *SEM conference and the CADIM Arabic Dialect Group. Her career spans academia and industry, including leadership roles at Meta and Amazon AWS. Her research connects multilingual NLP, cultural and social dimensions of meaning, and responsible AI. She advocates for a science of language, meaning, and interaction, with Arabic helping shape foundational questions about intelligence, understanding, and trustworthy AI.
