Dr. Areeb Alowisheq focuses on developing and managing research projects to build competing Arabic Language technologies. As Vice President of AI Research at HUMAIN and Head of HUMAIN Chat, she leads efforts to develop human-aligned generative and agentic technologies. Formally Assistant CEO for Research and Development at the National Center for AI at SDAIA, she leads the training of ALLAM and previously  SauTech programs, Saudi Arabia’s flagship LLM and speech initiatives. Previously an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Imam University, Areeb’s work bridges research, productization, and governance to advance a sustainable Arabic AI ecosystem.
